Privacy Policy - Personal Smart Spend

Effective Date: July 26, 2026
Last Updated: August 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Personal Smart Spend (the "App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Android application Personal Smart Spend (package: com.personalsmartspend.app) and our official privacy policy page.

Personal Smart Spend is a local-first personal finance app. Your financial records are intended to stay on your device unless you choose to back them up, export them, or share them.

Summary
  • Your financial records (transactions, budgets, goals, categories, notes, etc.) are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not upload your financial records to our servers.
  • The Smart Money Thought card selects one bundled entry using only the device-local calendar date. It does not use or transmit your financial records and does not require location or timezone permission.
  • If you choose Google Drive backup, the App uploads a profile-specific backup file directly to the Google account you select. You may also enable a once-daily backup for each active profile when the App opens or that profile is selected.
  • The App may use third-party services such as Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play billing, depending on your settings and consent choices.
  • Premium subscriptions can remove ads and unlock premium features.
  • You can manage privacy and consent choices inside the App under Settings > Privacy & Consent.

4. Local-First Data Handling

Personal Smart Spend is designed so that your financial records remain on your device by default.

The App may create or manage local files when you use features like:

  • Backup and restore
  • Optional manual or daily Google Drive backup
  • CSV export
  • PDF reports
  • File sharing (when you choose to share a file)

If the active profile has a profile photo, a backup may include a resized, metadata-stripped JPEG copy of that photo. The photo remains inside the backup file and is restored to app storage on the destination device.

If you connect Google Drive, the App requests the limited drive.file permission and can access only Drive files created by Personal Smart Spend. It creates a visible Personal Smart Spend Backups folder in the selected Google account and maintains a separate latest backup for each profile you back up. Manual backup runs when you request it. Daily automatic backup runs at most once per local calendar day for each active profile when the App opens, resumes, or that profile is selected, and only after you explicitly enable it.

The App processes the selected Google account name/email to show which account is connected and to authorize your chosen Drive operations. Google handles account sign-in and Drive storage under its own terms and privacy policy. The backup is transferred directly between the App and your Google Drive; it is not uploaded to our servers.

You may choose passphrase encryption for manual and automatic Drive backups. When you encrypt automatic backups, the App stores that passphrase in platform secure storage on the device so it can create the next backup. You must remember the passphrase to restore the encrypted backup on another device. Signing out disables automatic backup and removes the locally stored automatic-backup passphrase, but it does not delete a backup already stored in your Drive.

If you export or share files, those files may be stored or transmitted by the apps/services you choose (for example email, cloud storage, messaging apps). Their privacy policies will apply.

6. Premium Features and Payments

Personal Smart Spend may offer Premium subscriptions, such as 1 month, 3 months, and 1 year plans, through Google Play.

Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment card details.

For Android, the App asks Google Play for subscriptions owned by the currently signed-in purchasing account. It validates the signed purchase data locally using the app's Google Play licensing public key and acknowledges verified purchases through Google Play billing when required. The App does not send the purchase token or signed purchase JSON to a developer-operated billing server, and it does not use Firebase Functions for billing verification.

The App may locally store limited entitlement metadata and the signed Google Play purchase proof. An editable Premium flag does not independently grant access: the proof must have a valid Google Play signature. Premium may remain available offline for up to seven days after the last successful Google Play refresh. The App must then reconnect to Google Play; an online response that no longer returns the subscription removes Premium access.

We do not intentionally present third-party quotations or imply third-party endorsement. If you believe an entry raises an intellectual-property or other rights concern, contact help.personalsmartspend@gmail.com so it can be reviewed.

Smart Money Thought and Smart Insights provide general motivational and educational information only. They are not personalized financial, investment, legal, tax, or accounting advice; an offer or solicitation; or a promise or guarantee of any result. They do not recommend a specific security, investment product, lender, purchase, return, or outcome. You remain responsible for your decisions and should consider your own circumstances and consult an appropriately qualified professional when needed.
