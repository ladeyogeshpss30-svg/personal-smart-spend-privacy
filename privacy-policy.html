Privacy Policy - Personal Smart Spend

Effective Date: March 12, 2026
Last Updated: August 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Personal Smart Spend (the “App”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) accesses, collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information.

Personal Smart Spend is a local-first personal finance application. Most financial records and settings remain on your device. Information is transmitted off your device only when required for a feature you use, such as advertising, optional analytics, optional crash reporting, Google Play billing, support communications, or optional Google Drive backup.

Privacy Summary
  • Your transactions, budgets, goals, accounts, categories, profiles, reminders, and most settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a server that receives your financial ledger during normal use of the App.
  • Google Drive backup is optional and requires you to select a Google account and grant Drive access.
  • Optional Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collection are disabled until you enable them.
  • The free version may show Google AdMob advertisements after the applicable advertising-consent process permits ad requests.
  • Premium purchases are processed through Google Play. We do not collect your payment-card details.
  • We do not sell your personal or sensitive information.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Personal Smart Spend Android App, its in-app Privacy & Consent settings, optional Google Drive backup, support communications, and this hosted privacy-policy page.

This policy does not control services operated independently by Google or another application you choose when exporting, storing, or sharing information.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Financial and app information

The App may locally store information that you enter or generate, including:

  • Expenses, income, transfers, balances, and account records
  • Transaction dates, amounts, notes, labels, and descriptions
  • Categories, income sources, budgets, and alert thresholds
  • Financial goals and goal contributions
  • Recurring transaction rules and generated recurring entries
  • Profile names and profile-specific financial information
  • Dashboard layout, filters, tracking mode, and display settings
  • Reminder settings, notification history, and activity logs
  • Currency, month-start-day, and other app preferences
  • App-lock, hide-balance, and security preferences

Smart Insights and financial summaries are generated from records stored on your device. They are intended as informational app features and are not professional financial, legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice.

Profile photos

If you choose a profile photo, the App accesses the image you select through an Android picker and copies a managed version into local app storage. The App does not require broad access to your photo library.

When included in a backup, the profile image may be resized and re-encoded. Location and camera metadata are not intentionally included in the re-encoded backup image.

Security information

App-lock PIN material, lock settings, and an optional automatic-backup passphrase may be stored using device-protected or platform secure storage where supported.

App-lock PIN material and locally cached Premium-entitlement data are excluded from portable backups.

5. Google Drive Backup

Google Drive backup is optional. Connecting a Google account does not create a Personal Smart Spend developer-operated account.

The App requests the limited Google Drive drive.file permission. The current backup feature uses this permission only to create, identify, list, update, download, and restore Drive folders and backup files created by Personal Smart Spend.

The App creates a visible Personal Smart Spend Backups folder in the selected Google account and maintains a current profile-specific backup for each profile you back up. The App may also identify compatible older backups created by the App.

A backup may contain:

  • Your profile’s financial database
  • Profile-specific settings and preferences
  • Profile name and internal profile identifiers
  • A compressed profile photo, if one is configured
  • Backup format, creation time, and integrity information

Manual backup runs when you request it. Daily automatic backup runs only after you explicitly enable it. It may run at most once per local calendar day for each active profile when the App opens, resumes, or that profile is selected.

Backups are transferred directly between the App and the Google Drive account you select. We do not receive or view the backup through a developer-operated backup server.

Backup encryption

You may protect a manual or automatic backup with a passphrase. When selected, the backup content is encrypted on the device before upload. Some Drive file metadata, such as the file name, profile name, size, dates, and backup-status properties, remains available to Google Drive even when the backup content is encrypted.

If you enable encrypted automatic backups, the passphrase is stored in platform secure storage on your device so the App can create future backups. You must remember the passphrase to restore the backup on another device. We cannot recover a forgotten passphrase.

If you create a backup without passphrase encryption, anyone who obtains access to the backup file may be able to read its contents. You are responsible for securing unencrypted exported or backup files.

Signing out and deleting Drive backups

Signing out disables automatic backup, removes the automatic-backup passphrase from secure storage, and clears active Drive connection information. Some locally stored backup-history information, such as a previous backup date, file name, size, or last-used account email, may remain until the relevant profile or App data is deleted.

Signing out or uninstalling the App does not delete files already stored in Google Drive. To delete them, open Google Drive and delete the files or the Personal Smart Spend Backups folder. You may separately revoke the App’s Google access through your Google Account permissions.

Google API data-use commitment

Personal Smart Spend’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Premium Subscriptions and Google Play

Personal Smart Spend may offer Premium subscriptions through Google Play, including monthly or other recurring plans shown in the App.

For Android purchases, the App asks Google Play for purchases owned by the currently signed-in Play account. It validates Google Play-signed purchase information locally using the App’s licensing public key and acknowledges eligible purchases when required.

The App does not send purchase tokens or signed purchase JSON to a developer-operated billing-verification server.

The App may locally store:

  • Whether Premium is active
  • The active product identifier
  • The last verification time
  • Google Play-signed purchase JSON, signature, and purchase token required for local verification and limited offline restoration

Premium may remain available for a limited offline period based on a previously verified Google Play purchase. The App must periodically reconnect to Google Play to refresh the purchase state.

Uninstalling the App or deleting local App data does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription. Subscriptions can be managed through Google Play.

The App uses Android picker flows where possible and is configured to avoid broad photo, video, audio, or external-storage read permissions. Runtime permissions are requested in context when required by Android.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Local App data

Local records remain on your device until you delete individual records, delete a profile, reset App data, overwrite data through a restore, clear the App’s storage through Android settings, or uninstall the App.

Deleting one profile removes that profile and its managed local data from the device. It does not delete backup files previously exported or uploaded to Google Drive.

Google Drive backups

Google Drive backups remain in the selected Google account until they are replaced or deleted. Signing out, deleting a local profile, resetting the App, or uninstalling the App does not delete the Drive copy.

Exported and shared files

Backup, CSV, PDF, and other exported files remain wherever you save or share them until you delete them from that location or service.

Analytics, advertising, crash, and billing data

Information processed by Google is retained according to Google’s applicable product settings, retention controls, legal requirements, and privacy policies. Disabling an optional feature stops future developer-requested collection through that feature but may not immediately delete information already retained by Google.

Support communications

Support emails and attachments may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer your request, maintain support records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or comply with legal obligations. You may contact us to request deletion of support information under our control, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

12. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures appropriate to the App, including:

  • Android application sandbox storage
  • Disabled Android system backup for internal App data
  • Platform secure storage for supported security information
  • Optional local app PIN and device authentication
  • Optional passphrase encryption for portable backups
  • Integrity checks for backup files
  • Modern encrypted network connections such as HTTPS/TLS
  • Limited Google Drive authorization through drive.file

Local financial database records are not represented as being independently encrypted merely because they are stored in the Android application sandbox. Device-level protection, such as a secure screen lock and device encryption, remains important.

No electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure. You should protect your device, Google account, backup files, and backup passphrases.
